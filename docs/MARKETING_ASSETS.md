## 7. Ad Creative Direction

### Visual Style
- Clean, modern, minimal
- Use the brand blue (#2457d6) as the primary accent
- Dark backgrounds with light text for video/reels
- Real Kenyan students where possible (not stock photos)
- Screenshots of the actual chat interface work well for social proof

### Video Content
- Screen recordings of the AI chat in action (most effective)
- Screen recordings of the "Access fit" flow
- Talking head videos explaining CBC pathway rules (educational content)
- Parent testimonial format: "I didn't realise my child's Grade 8 marks counted until I used this"

### Static Ad Formats
- Single image: Chat interface screenshot with "Free AI Career Counsellor for CBC Students" overlay
- Carousel: "3 Questions Before You Pick Your CBC Pathway" (see above)
- Comparison: STEM vs Social Sciences vs Arts side by side

## 8. Target Audience Profiles

### Primary: CBC Parents (Ages 35-55)
- Concerned about their child's CBC pathway choice
- Don't fully understand the new system
- Want to make sure their child doesn't "pick wrong"
- Responsive to educational content and urgency messaging
- Platforms: Facebook, WhatsApp, Email

### Secondary: CBC Students (Ages 13-18, Grades 7-12)
- Exploring career options independently
- Want quick answers, not long forms
- Comfortable with AI and chat interfaces
- Responsive to peer recommendations and short-form video
- Platforms: Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp

### Tertiary: Career Counsellors & Teachers
- Looking for tools to support their students
- Interested in the counselor booking feature
- Platforms: LinkedIn, Twitter, Email
